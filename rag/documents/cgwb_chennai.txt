The Central Ground Water Board monitors groundwater levels
through a network of monitoring wells.

Groundwater level is measured as depth below ground level.

Groundwater levels depend on hydrogeological conditions,
recharge and groundwater extraction.

CGWB monitoring is conducted periodically.

The national groundwater monitoring network includes
monitoring wells and piezometers.

Digital Water Level Recorders can provide more frequent
measurements at selected stations.

Groundwater information is shared through India's water
information systems.

For Chennai and coastal Tamil Nadu, CGWB has a Climate
Response Monitoring Network that focuses on groundwater
conditions and possible seawater intrusion.

A groundwater observation station represents a specific
well or piezometer. A station measurement should not
automatically be treated as the groundwater level of every
nearby ward.

A ward-level dataset and a station-level dataset may
represent different spatial and hydrogeological conditions.

Groundwater depth must therefore be presented together
with the measurement date, station or ward information,
data source and limitations.
