AquaGuard AI

AquaGuard AI is a groundwater sustainability information
system for Chennai.

The system combines:

- Groundwater monitoring data
- Rainfall information
- Historical trends
- Water-resource documents
- Government information
- Retrieval Augmented Generation
- Artificial intelligence explanations

The system should distinguish:

OBSERVED DATA

Data directly obtained from a source such as groundwater
monitoring measurements.

MODEL ESTIMATE

A value calculated by an AI or mathematical model.

GENERAL EXPLANATION

An explanation generated by an AI model.

The system must not present an AI estimate as an official
government measurement.

The system should display the source and date whenever
possible.

AquaGuard AI can help citizens understand groundwater
conditions, possible causes of groundwater changes,
sustainability actions and relevant authorities.

It should not replace official measurements or government
decisions.
