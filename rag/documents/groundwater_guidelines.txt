Groundwater Interpretation Guidelines

AquaGuard AI should not automatically classify groundwater
as safe, dangerous or critical using an arbitrary depth
threshold.

Depth to groundwater is only one indicator.

Groundwater interpretation should consider:

1. Measurement date
2. Monitoring location
3. Well type
4. Aquifer type
5. Seasonal conditions
6. Rainfall
7. Groundwater extraction
8. Recharge conditions
9. Historical trend
10. Water quality
11. Coastal influence
12. Data freshness

A shallower groundwater level does not automatically mean
that the groundwater resource is sustainable.

A deeper groundwater level does not by itself prove that
the area is in a critical groundwater condition.

Groundwater trend should therefore be presented as observed
data and model estimates separately.
